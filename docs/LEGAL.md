# Content and legal boundaries

This project is a catalog/reference scaffold. It does not implement protected stream extraction, DRM circumvention, access-control bypass, or unauthorized media downloading.

Use the plugin only with:
- media you created or own,
- media you are licensed to distribute or stream,
- public-domain or openly licensed media,
- user-supplied playlist/stream URLs where you have permission.

Third-party site names and domains are references only. Their content, trademarks, logos, and terms remain owned by their respective holders. Users are responsible for compliance with applicable laws and site terms.
